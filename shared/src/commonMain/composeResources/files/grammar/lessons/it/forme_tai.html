Esprimere il Desiderio: ~Tai (~たい)

The ~tai form is used to express what the speaker vuole fare. It is only used for your own desires or when asking someone else directly what they want.

1. Come coniugare

The ~tai ending attaches to the Gambo del verbo (the part before ~masu).

Gruppo 1: Verbi in U (Godan)

Change the final "u" to "i" and add たい.

  • 行く (iku) → 行き (iki) → 行きたい (ikitai) - Want to go
  • 飲む (nomu) → 飲み (nomi) → 飲みたい (nomitai) - Want to drink

Gruppo 2: Verbi Ru (Ichidan)

Remove the final る (ru) and add たい.

  • 食べる (taberu) → 食べたい (tabetai) - Want to eat
  • 見る (miru) → 見たい (mitai) - Want to see

Gruppo 3: Verbi irregolari

  • する (suru) → したい (shitai) - Want to do
  • 来る (kuru) → 来たい (kitai) - Want to come

2. Si comporta come un aggettivo I!

Once you add ~tai, the word conjugates exactly like an I-adjective.

  • Affermativa: 行きたいです (I want to go)
  • Negativo: 行きたくないです (I don't want to go)
  • Passato: 行きたかったです (I wanted to go)
  • Passato negativo: 行きたくなかったです (I didn't want to go)

3. Utilizzo delle particelle

With ~tai, you can use either を or が to mark the object, though が often emphasizes the object of desire.

  • 水を飲みたい。(Mizu wo nomitai) - Voglio bere acqua.
  • 水が飲みたい。(Mizu ga nomitai) - I want to drink acqua (specifically).
