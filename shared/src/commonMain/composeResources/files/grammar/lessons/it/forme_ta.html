La forma Ta (た形) - Passato informale

The Forma Ta is used for the past tense in casual Japanese. It is essentially the "Past Tense" equivalent of the Dictionary Form.

1. Come coniugare

Buone notizie! If you already know the Te-form, you already know the Ta-form. The rules are identical: just replace te/de with ta/da.

Gruppo 1: Verbi in U (Godan)

Fine Cambia in... Esempio (Dizionario → Ta)
う (u), つ (tsu), る (ru) ～った (-tta) 買った (katta) - Comprato
む (mu), ぶ (bu), ぬ (nu) ～んだ (-nda) 読んだ (yonda) - Leggi
く (ku) ～いた (-ita) 書いた (kaita) - Ha scritto
ぐ (gu) ～いだ (-ida) 泳いだ (oyoida) - Nuotare
す (su) ～した (-shita) 話した (hanashita) - Ha parlato

⚠️ Eccezione: 行く (iku) becomes 行った (itta).

Gruppo 2: Verbi Ru (Ichidan)

Drop the final る (ru) and add た (ta).

  • 食べる (taberu) → 食べた (tabeta) - Ate
  • 見る (miru) → 見た (mita) - Saw

Gruppo 3: Verbi irregolari

  • する (suru) → した (shita) - Did
  • 来る (kuru) → 来た (kita) - Came

2. Modelli grammaticali comuni

  1. Esperienza passata (~koto ga aru): "I have done something before."
    日本に行ったことがあります。(Nihon ni itta koto ga arimasu) - I have been to Japan.
  2. Dare consigli (~hou ga ii): "It's better to do..."
    薬を飲んだほうがいいです。(Kusuri wo nonda hou ga ii desu) - You should take medicine.
  3. Azioni di elenco (~tari ~tari suru): "Doing things like A and B."
    本を読んだり、音楽を聞いたりします。(Hon wo yondari, ongaku wo kikutari shimasu) - I do things like read books and listen to music.
