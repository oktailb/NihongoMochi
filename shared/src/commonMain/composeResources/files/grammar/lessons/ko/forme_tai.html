욕망 표현 : ~Tai (~たい)

The ~타이 form is used to express what the speaker 하고 싶어. It is only used for your own desires or when asking someone else directly what they want.

1. 활용방법

The ~타이 ending attaches to the 동사 줄기 (the part before ~마스).

그룹 1: U-동사(Godan)

Change the final "u" to "i" and add たい.

  • 行く (iku) → 行き (iki) → 行木たい (ikitai) - Want to go
  • 飲む (nomu) → 飲み (nomi) → 飲またい (nomitai) - Want to drink

그룹 2: Ru-동사(Ichidan)

Remove the final 루(루) and add たい.

  • 食べる (taberu) → 식베타이 (tabetai) - Want to eat
  • 見る (miru) → 見たい (mitai) - Want to see

그룹 3: 불규칙 동사

  • する (suru) → 샤타이 (shitai) - Want to do
  • 来る (kuru) → 来たい (kitai) - Want to come

2. I-형용사처럼 행동합니다!

Once you add ~타이, the word conjugates exactly like an I-adjective.

  • 긍정: 行きたいです (I want to go)
  • 부정적인: 行きた쿠나이です (I don't want to go)
  • 과거: 行きた가타です (I wanted to go)
  • 과거 부정문: 行きた쿠나카타です (I didn't want to go)

3. 파티클 사용법

With ~타이, you can use either 을 or が to mark the object, though が often emphasizes the object of desire.

  • 水を飲umiたい.(Mizu wo nomitai) - 물을 마시고 싶어요.
  • 水が飲みたい。(Mizu 가 nomitai) - I want to drink 물 (specifically).
