명사화(이것과 の)

In Japanese, you can turn a verb or an entire sentence into a "noun" so that it can be used as a subject, object, or with other particles. This process is called 명목화.

1. と(koto) 사용하기

이것 means "thing" (intangible). Adding it after a verb in the dictionary form turns it into "the act of doing...".

  • 식베루(taberu) - 먹다
  • 食べる이것 (taberu koto) - Eating / The act of eating

일반적인 패턴:

  • ...이것으로 합시다 (...koto ga dekiru) - Can do something (lit: The act of doing is possible).
    日本語を話す이것ができます。(Nihongo wo hanasu koto ga dekiru) - I can speak Japanese.
  • 趣味は...이것입니다 (Shumi wa... koto desu) - My hobby is...
    私の趣味は写真を撮る이것です。(Watashi no shumi wa shashin wo toru koto desu) - My hobby is taking photos.

2. の (아니오) 사용

の is more casual and versatile than 이것. It is often used with verbs of perception (seeing, hearing) or with specific phrases.

  • 歌うのを聞きました。(Utau no wo kikimashita) - I heard [someone] singing.
  • 泳ぐのが好きです。(Oyogu no ga suki desu) - I like swimming.

3. Koto vs No: 언제 어느 것을 사용할 것인가?

Use 와(koto) for... Use の (아니요) for...
공식적인 정의 개인적인 감정(좋아요/싫어요)
문법 수정(고토가루 등) 지각 동사(보고, 듣고, 느끼다)
문장의 끝에서 (데스) 작업 대기 또는 중지

4. 요약 예시

日本に行く이것にしました。(Nihon ni iku koto ni shimashita) - I decided to go to Japan (lit: Decided on the 'thing' of going to Japan).
