Mengekspresikan Keinginan: ~Tai (~たい)

The ~tai form is used to express what the speaker ingin lakukan. It is only used for your own desires or when asking someone else directly what they want.

1. Cara Konjugasi

The ~tai ending attaches to the Batang Kata Kerja (the part before ~masu).

Kelompok 1: U-Verba (Godan)

Change the final "u" to "i" and add たい.

  • 行く (iku) → 行き (iki) → 行きたい (ikitai) - Want to go
  • 飲む (nomu) → 飲み (nomi) → 飲みたい (nomitai) - Want to drink

Grup 2: Kata Kerja Ru (Ichidan)

Remove the final る (ru) and add たい.

  • 食べる (taberu) → 食べたい (tabetai) - Want to eat
  • 見る (miru) → itu (mitai) - Want to see

Kelompok 3: Kata Kerja Tidak Beraturan

  • する (suru) → したい (shitai) - Want to do
  • 来る (kuru) → たい (kitai) - Want to come

2. Berperilaku seperti Kata Sifat-I!

Once you add ~tai, the word conjugates exactly like an I-adjective.

  • Setuju: 行きたいです (I want to go)
  • Negatif: 行きたくないです (I don't want to go)
  • Masa lalu: 行きたかったです (I wanted to go)
  • Negatif Sebelumnya: 行きたくなかったです (I didn't want to go)

3. Penggunaan Partikel

With ~tai, you can use either を or が to mark the object, though が often emphasizes the object of desire.

  • 水を飲みたい。(Mizu wo nomitai) - Saya ingin minum air.
  • 水が飲みたい。(Mizu ga nomitai) - I want to drink air (specifically).
