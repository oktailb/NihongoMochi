The Te-form (て形) - The "Swiss Army Knife" of Japanese

The te-form is a versatile verb form used to connect sentences, describe ongoing actions, or make requests. It doesn't have a tense of its own; it inherits the tense of the final verb in the sentence.

1. How to Conjugate

Formation depends on which group the verb belongs to. Mastering these rules is essential for your progress!

Group 1: U-Verbs (Godan)

The ending of the dictionary form determines the change:

Ending Change to... Example (Dictionary → Te)
う (u), つ (tsu), る (ru) ～って (-tte) 買う (kau) → 買って (katte)
む (mu), ぶ (bu), ぬ (nu) ～んで (-nde) 読む (yomu) → 読んで (yonde)
く (ku) ～いて (-ite) 書く (kaku) → 書いて (kaite)
ぐ (gu) ～いで (-ide) 泳ぐ (oyogu) → 泳いで (oyoide)
す (su) ～して (-shite) 話す (hanasu) → 話して (hanashite)

⚠️ Exception: The verb 行く (iku - to go) becomes 行って (itte).

Group 2: Ru-Verbs (Ichidan)

These are the easiest! Just drop the final る (ru) and add て (te).

  • 食べる (taberu) → 食べて (tabete)
  • 見る (miru) → 見て (mite)
  • 寝る (neru) → 寝て (nete)

Group 3: Irregular Verbs

There are only two, so simply memorize them:

  • する (suru - to do) → して (shite)
  • 来る (kuru - to come) → 来て (kite)

2. Why is it useful?

Once you know the te-form, you can build many useful expressions:

  1. Connecting Actions: Listing things you do in order.
    Ex: 朝起きて、コーヒーを飲みます。 (I wake up and drink coffee.)
  2. Requests (~te kudasai): Asking someone to do something.
    Ex: 日本語で話してください。 (Please speak in Japanese.)
  3. Ongoing Actions (~te iru): Describing what is happening right now.
    Ex: 今、勉強しています。 (I am studying right now.)
  4. Sequence (~te kara): Saying "After doing X, I do Y".
    Ex: 手を洗ってから、食べます。 (After washing my hands, I eat.)
