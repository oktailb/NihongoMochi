Verlangen ausdrücken: ~Tai (~たい)

The ~tai form is used to express what the speaker will tun. It is only used for your own desires or when asking someone else directly what they want.

1. Wie man konjugiert

The ~tai ending attaches to the Verbstamm (the part before ~masu).

Gruppe 1: U-Verben (Godan)

Change the final "u" to "i" and add たい.

  • 行く (iku) → 行き (iki) → 行きたい (ikitai) - Want to go
  • 飲む (nomu) → 飲み (nomi) → 飲みたい (nomitai) - Want to drink

Gruppe 2: Ru-Verben (Ichidan)

Remove the final る (ru) and add たい.

  • 食べる (taberu) → 食べたい (tabetai) - Want to eat
  • 見る (miru) → 見たい (mitai) - Want to see

Gruppe 3: Unregelmäßige Verben

  • する (suru) → したい (shitai) - Want to do
  • 来る (kuru) → Nein (kitai) - Want to come

2. Es verhält sich wie ein Ich-Adjektiv!

Once you add ~tai, the word conjugates exactly like an I-adjective.

  • Positiv: 行きたいです (I want to go)
  • Negativ: 行きたくないです (I don't want to go)
  • Vergangenheit: 行きたかったです (I wanted to go)
  • Vergangenes Negativ: 行きたくなかったです (I didn't want to go)

3. Partikelnutzung

With ~tai, you can use either を or が to mark the object, though が often emphasizes the object of desire.

  • 水を飲みたい。(Mizu wo nomitai) – Ich möchte Wasser trinken.
  • 水が飲みたい。(Mizu ga nomitai) - I want to drink Wasser (specifically).
