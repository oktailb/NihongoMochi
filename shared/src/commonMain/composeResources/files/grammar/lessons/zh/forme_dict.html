词典形式（辞书形）

The 词典形式 is the most basic form of a Japanese verb. It is the form you will find when looking up words in a dictionary. It is also used for casual speech among friends and family.

1. 三个动词组

每个日语动词都属于三个组之一。了解该基团对于任何进一步的结合至关重要。

第一组：U-动词（Godan）

These verbs always end in an “你” sound in their dictionary form.

  • 買う (kau) - to buy
  • 待つ (matsu) - to wait
  • 行く (iku) - to go
  • 飲む (nomu) - to drink
  • 話す (hanasu) - to speak

第 2 组：Ru-动词（Ichidan）

These verbs always end in る (ru), preceded by an "i" or "e" sound.

  • 食べる (taberu) - to eat
  • 見る (miru) - to see/watch
  • 寝る (neru) - to sleep

第三组：不规则动词

日语中主要的不规则动词只有两个：

  • する (suru) - to do
  • 来る (kuru) - to come

2. 随意使用

In casual Japanese, the dictionary form is used to express the 现在肯定句 or the 未来.

  • 明日、日本に行く。 (Ashita, nihon ni iku.) - Tomorrow, I will go to Japan.
  • 毎日、水を饮む。 (Mainichi, mizu wo nomu.) - Every day, I drink water.

3. 语法结构

许多常见语法模式都需要字典形式：

  • ...ことができる (...koto ga dekiru) - Can do something.
  • ...つもりです (...tsumori desu) - Plan/Intend to do something.
  • ...前に (...mae ni) - Before doing something.
