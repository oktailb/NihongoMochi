正在进行的行动和状态：~Te Iru (~ている)

The 〜特伊鲁 structure is one of the most frequently used patterns in Japanese. Depending on the verb, it describes either an action currently in progress or a lasting state resulting from a past action.

1. 如何形成

Take the Te型 of any verb and add the verb いる (iru - to exist/be).

  • 読む (yomu) → 読んで (yonde) → 読んでいる (yonde iru) - Reading
  • 食べる (taberu) → 食べて (tabete) → 食べている (tabete iru) - Eating

2. 两个主要含义

A. 正在进行的行动（持续）

With "action verbs" like eating, reading, or running, it means you are doing it 现在.

  • 今、本を読んでいます。(Ima, hon wo yonde imasu) - I am reading a book now.

B. 结果状态

With "change-of-state verbs" like getting married, waking up, or sitting down, it describes a 继续的状态 after the action happened.

  • 結婚しています。(Kekkon shite imasu) - I am married (lit: I have married and am in that state).
  • 知っています。(Shitte imasu) - I know (lit: I have learned and now possess the knowledge).
  • 窓が閉まっています。(Mado ga shimatte imasu) - The window is closed.

3. 随意与礼貌

You can conjugate 伊鲁 to match the politeness level you need:

  • 随意的： 食べている (tabete iru)
  • 有礼貌的： 食べています (tabete imasu)
  • 休闲过去： 食べていた (tabete ita)
  • 礼貌的过去： 食べていました (tabete imashita)

4. 随意的“i”掉落

In casual spoken Japanese, the い（一） is often dropped.

  • 何してるの？ (Nani shite茹 no?) - What are you doing? (instead of 什特伊鲁)
