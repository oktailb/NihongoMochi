Listing Actions: ~Tari ~Tari (~たり ~たり)

The ~tari ~tari suru structure is used to list two or more actions from a larger set of activities. It implies "doing things like A and B (among others)".

1. How to Form It

Take the Ta-form (informal past) of the verb and add り (ri). Always end the sentence with the verb する (suru) to indicate the tense and politeness level.

Examples:

  • 読む (yomu - read) → 読んだ (yonda) → 読んだり (yondari)
  • 聞く (kiku - listen) → 聞いた (kiita) → 聞いたり (kiitari)

2. Usage in a Sentence

[Action A]たり、[Action B]たり します。

  • 休みの日は、映画を見たり、本を読んだりします。(Yasumi no hi wa, eiga wo mitari, hon wo yondari shimasu) - On my days off, I do things like watch movies and read books.
  • 昨日は、買い物をしたり、友達に会ったりしました。(Kinou wa, kaimono wo shitari, tomodachi ni attari shimashita) - Yesterday, I did things like shopping and meeting friends.

3. Important Differences

Structure Meaning Nuance
～て、～て (Te-form) A, then B Sequential order (exhaustive)
～たり、～たり (Tari-form) A and B (etc.) Non-exhaustive list (random order)

4. Adjectives and Nouns

You can also use this with adjectives and nouns (though it's less common for N5):

  • 暑かったり、寒かったりします。(Atsukattari, samukattari shimasu) - Sometimes it's hot, sometimes it's cold.
